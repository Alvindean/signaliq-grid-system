Frequently Asked Questions

What types of organizations does AHRA work with?

Museums, historical societies, libraries, universities, K-12 schools, law firms, cultural nonprofits, and media companies across the United States.

How does AHRA approach archival consulting?

We combine rigorous scholarship with community-centered practice, ensuring organizations have both the expertise and the accessibility they need.

Does AHRA offer curriculum development?

Yes. Dr. Dixon has developed African American history curriculum for the state of Florida and offers curriculum development services for schools and districts.

What is AHRA's role in the Black Seminole Project?

AHRA serves as the production company behind both the documentary and feature film, ensuring historical accuracy and scholarly rigor throughout development.

How can I request a consultation?

Use our contact form or email inquiries@profaedixon.com. We respond within 2 business days.
