Three Books. One Mission.

Explore Dr. Dixon's published works on African American history, resilience, and public memory.

UP FROM
INCARCERATION
Anthony E. Dixon
2022

Up From Incarceration

A transformative memoir chronicling Dr. Dixon's extraordinary journey from Florida state prison to earning his PhD from Indiana University. This powerful narrative explores education as liberation, resilience in the face of systemic barriers, and the possibility of second chances. A profound testament to human potential and the redemptive power of scholarship.

FLORIDA'S
NEGRO WAR
Anthony E. Dixon
2014

Florida's Negro War

The definitive scholarly work on the Black Seminoles and the Second Seminole War (1835-1842). Dr. Dixon's comprehensive research reveals the often-overlooked stories of African American resistance, sovereignty, and cultural persistence in Florida history. Essential reading for understanding the complexity of early American racial politics and the agency of enslaved and free Black communities.

A PENNY FOR
YOUR THOUGHTS
Anthony E. Dixon
2013

A Penny for Your Thoughts

A thoughtful collection of essays and reflections exploring the intersections of history, identity, education, and the African American experience. Through personal narrative and historical analysis, Dr. Dixon examines how we construct meaning from the past and the responsibility historians bear in preserving and interpreting collective memory.

Why I Write

"Writing is an act of remembrance and resistance. In archives and through scholarship, we reclaim narratives that have been suppressed, forgotten, or deliberately erased. My books are not just academic works—they are acts of witness, preservation, and love for the communities whose stories deserve to be told with honesty and dignity. Every page is written with the conviction that history belongs to everyone, and that understanding our past is essential to building a more just future."

— Dr. Anthony E. Dixon
