The Scholar. The Speaker. The Story.

An unconventional path from Florida state prison to a PhD—and a lifetime dedicated to bringing African American history to light.

"Education did not just change my life — it gave me one."

Dr. Anthony E. Dixon is a public historian, author, and keynote speaker whose life story embodies the transformative power of education and second chances. Once incarcerated in Florida state prison, Dr. Dixon earned his freedom through extraordinary academic achievement, ultimately earning his PhD from Indiana University.

Today, he is the founder and director of the Archival and Historical Research Associates (AHRA), a dedicated organization advancing the study and preservation of African American history. With a focus on the Black Seminole narrative, criminal justice reform, and public memory, Dr. Dixon has published three groundbreaking books and delivered keynotes to universities, museums, and organizations across the nation.

His research, writing, and speaking have earned him recognition as one of the leading voices in African American public history. Learn more about his published works and the mission of AHRA.
