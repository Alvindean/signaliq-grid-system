History is not just about the past. It's about who gets to tell it.

Dr. Anthony E. Dixon's journey from incarceration to earning a PhD from Indiana University is a testament to the transformative power of education and historical scholarship. Over twenty years, he has dedicated himself to uncovering forgotten stories and bringing them to light.

His work spans three published books, keynote speaking engagements across the nation, and the founding of Archival and Historical Research Associates (AHRA) in 2009. Dr. Dixon believes that public history is not a luxury—it's a necessity for understanding who we are as a nation.

Archival & Historical Research Associates

Founded in 2009, AHRA provides research, publishing, and media services rooted in rigorous scholarship and public engagement.

◆

Consultancy

Expert research and historical consultation for institutions, municipalities, and organizations seeking to understand and present their own stories authentically.
