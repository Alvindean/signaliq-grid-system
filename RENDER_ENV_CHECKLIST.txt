Render Environment Checklist

Required for app/auth:
JWT_SECRET=<generate a long random value>
ADMIN_EMAIL=thealvindean@gmail.com
PORT=3000
NODE_ENV=production
SERVE_STATIC=true

Required after Render gives you a live URL:
APP_ORIGIN=https://<your-render-or-custom-domain>
PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://<your-render-or-custom-domain>
API_PUBLIC_URL=https://<your-render-or-custom-domain>
GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI=https://<your-render-or-custom-domain>/api/google/callback

Required for live sending/tracking:
RESEND_API_KEY=<your current Resend API key>
RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET=<your current Resend webhook secret>
RESEND_WEBHOOK_KEY=<fallback random key, optional if signed webhook works>
CAMPAIGN_REPLY_TO=thealvindean@gmail.com
SENDING_FROM=SignalIQ <onboarding@resend.dev>

Required for conversion tracking:
CRM_WEBHOOK_KEY=crm_wh_a83efeda1510d56cba

Required for Google Workspace:
GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=<your Google client id>
GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET=<your Google client secret>

Optional for paid launch:
STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=
STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET=
STRIPE_PRO_MONTHLY_PRICE_ID=
STRIPE_TEAM_MONTHLY_PRICE_ID=

Post-deploy provider URLs:
Resend webhook: https://<your-render-or-custom-domain>/api/webhooks/resend
CRM conversion webhook: https://<your-render-or-custom-domain>/api/webhooks/conversions?key=<CRM_WEBHOOK_KEY>
Google OAuth redirect: https://<your-render-or-custom-domain>/api/google/callback
